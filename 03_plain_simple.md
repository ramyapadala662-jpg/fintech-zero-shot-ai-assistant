# Step 4: Plain Chat on a Simple Question

**Project:** FinBuddy – Plain Chat vs System Prompt  
**Course:** Prompt Engineering, AI & Machine Learning | BBA (FinTech), Chitkara Business School  
**Tool used:** Claude (Anthropic)  

## Set-up: Plain chat (new chat, no system prompt)

```text
Explain what a savings account is to a first-year college student in 3 short lines.
```

## AI output

A savings account is a bank account where you keep your money safely and earn a little interest.
You can add money, withdraw it and pay using UPI or a debit card whenever you need.
It helps you save for goals while keeping your savings separate from daily spending.

## What I noticed

The answer is clear, correct and exactly three short lines. A general question with no business rules does not need any set-up, so a plain chat is enough here.
